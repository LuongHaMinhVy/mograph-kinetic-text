# Motion spec: pop-collage-light (ref-ca-ngay-light.mp4)

Source: 10 contact sheets (cells 240x180 = 768x576 / 3.2), `metrics.json`, `cuts_atlas_01.png`. 555 frames @30 fps (18.5 s), 768x576.
All numbers are read off small cells: positions and scales are +-15 %; frame numbers are exact unless marked "~". (?) = unclear.
Percentages are of frame height (576 px) unless stated. Rotation sign: negative = counter-clockwise (text rises to the right).

## (b) Reusable motion vocabulary (30 fps)

1. **hard-cut-zoom-in** - jump to ~2.7x on a new anchor, no transition; land ~1.3x too big and ease back over 3-4 f (ease-out). f21, f154 (2.7x), f69 (1.9x).
2. **hard-cut-zoom-out** - jump to 0.3-0.5x. f53 0.47x, f166 0.5x, f183 0.3x. The previous giant word becomes a small word in the new frame.
3. **cut-rhythm** - 15-17 f between cuts (range 12-22) for ~6 s, ~11 cuts, then stop cutting and switch to morphing.
4. **push-in-punch** - after a cut, x2.0 in 3 f (ease-out cubic), then x1.3 in ~11 f (near linear), total ~2.6x in 14 f (f38-52). Cheap version: constant +3 %/f for 15 f.
5. **typewriter-voice** - letters appear instantly (no pop, no fade). 1-1.5 f/letter in bursts for hero words; 3-6 f/word for tiny sentences; 6-9 f/word for the smallest text. 5-f pauses inside a word are normal (f10 -> f15). Accent arrives with its letter, swaps happen (`ồ` -> `ô`).
6. **sticker-pop-whole** - coloured rectangle (3-10 deg tilt) appears in 1 f behind its first letter, no scale tween (f9, f35).
7. **sticker-grow-from-hairline** - rectangle starts as a 1-3 px bar, scales to full over 10-18 f (ease-in-out); text is typed into it once ~25 % open (f145-153, f271-291, f284-298). Palette: orange ~#F05A28, violet ~#5500E0, mint ~#8FD0A0, blue ~#3F55E8, red ~#D0102A, gold ~#E8B030.
8. **left-first-delete** - letters leave from the left, 1 letter per 3-4 f (stragglers up to 8-10 f); sticker clips/shrinks with its text and ends as a 3-12 px bar lingering 5-10 f (f61, f199-209, f303-324, f425-453).
9. **hollow-outline-type** - white fill, ~1 px grey (#888) stroke, no shadow, 20-35 % h font size, drawn over the flower; alternates with solid words (f3-8, f53-67, f103-139, f375-454). One solid letter inside a hollow word is allowed (`A` in `HAY`).
10. **slam-word** - one giant red word (cap ~55 %, font ~75 % h) typed 1 letter/f over 2-3 f (f88-90), holds ~12 f while tilting -2 -> -10 deg and zooming out ~0.85x.
11. **roll-settle** - group rotates -35 -> -5 deg over 20 f (ease-out) while scaling 1.0 -> 1.7x (f118-139). Entrance angles seen: -25, +30, -35, -12, -10 deg; settled tilt 0 to -8 deg.
12. **bg-flip-wipe** - 1-frame hard vertical wipe white -> black (f139 swell, f140 97 % black + 5 px white strip, f141 full); black ~#050005 for 131 f; return is a plain hard cut (f271).
13. **echo-trail** - repeat one glyph (`O`) 6x at ~75 % advance, hollow stroke only, one new copy per 3-6 f; exit left-first at ~4 f per copy; small solid text sits on top.
14. **decor-grow-and-fade** - badge appears whole, grows with the camera, shrinks to a plain dot and dims to black over ~10 f (f209-226); flowers get 6-10 px blur at large scale.
15. **sentence-wipe-tail** - tiny sentence deleted in one frame except last word (f503), last word 7 f later (f510), signature word `ngochuy` 11 f later (f521), then 33 empty frames.

## 7. Scale and layout (percent of 576 px frame height)

| Role | Examples | Cap height | Approx font size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Giant crop hero | `SAO` f90, `NAY` f21-37, `NHIÊU` f170, `HOA` f156, `Hay` f360 | 25-55 % | 35-75 % |
| Medium word | `nghĩ lời`, `như` sticker text, `thế này` hollow, `BÉ` | 12-20 % | 18-28 % |
| Wide intro | `NGÀY` f20, `cả` | 8-10 % | 12-14 % |
| Small | `lai loay`, `HIÚ chân`, `đôi`, `khẽ`, `của` | 3-7 % | 5-10 % |
| Micro | `la`, `vào đời làm sao`, `liệu ban đêm`..., `ngochuy`, `TAY` | 2-4 % | 3-5 % (~20 px) |

- Hero vs micro contrast is 10-20x, often in the SAME frame (f160 `HOAY` vs `lai loay`; f170 `NHIU` vs `ai`, `đô`).
- Cropping: hero words are cropped by the frame edge in most giant frames (`NGAY` top f21-34, `NAY` bottom f38-52, `N` left). Exact share not counted. Hollow words are cropped mainly by the scroll (f406-424).
- Text overlaps stickers by design (letters inside the sticker, mint over violet), flowers, and other words (`NHIU` over `HOAY`, `SAO` over the blue `LÀ` sticker, `BÉ` over `AM`).
- Z-order back to front: flower raster / photo -> big colour blocks -> sunburst badges -> solid text -> hollow text -> hairline doodles -> micro text. Exception: red `SAO` covers almost everything; stickers carry their own text.
- Text colour: black on white; white with a 1 px dark edge on stickers, flowers and black; blue `NGÀY`; orange `nghĩ lời`; red `SAO`. Font: bold Helvetica/Arial-style grotesque for solids, regular weight for hollow, bold ~20 px for the tiny sentence.
- Rotations: most text 0 +-5 deg; stickers 3-10 deg; showpiece rolls -25 (f86), +30 (`LÀ` f86), -35 -> -5 (`HAY` f118-139), -12 (tiny text f240+, hand), -10 (`SAO` f101, `BÉ`).

## Surprises / caveats
- The video stops hard-cutting after f183; the last ~370 frames are slow morphs, 131 of them dark.
- The black section is not an inverted white section: decor fades to near-empty (f217-236) and carries tiny text for ~50 f.
- Typewriter is voice-timed, irregular, letters instant (no per-letter scale pop observed); stickers pop whole or grow from a hairline; deletion is always left-first.
- Jump-in cuts are ~2.7x, jump-out cuts ~0.3-0.5x.
- Cells are small, so sub-frame easing is inferred from 3-4 samples (the 1.3x -> 1.0x settle after cuts, the 2.0x-in-3-f punch): treat as +-20 %. Rotation of the f86 composition and the f88 "cut" are the least certain items.
